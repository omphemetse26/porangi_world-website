Our Story

Porangi World was founded in 2021 with a clear vision: to use food as a bridge between cultures. We noticed that people were craving aunthentic, global flavors but didn't always have the means to travel. So we brought the world to Alexandra. Our goal was to create a welcoming place where you can travel the world without leaving our seats. We carefully source recipes from around the globe and prepare them with fresh,local ingredients. What started as a small kitchen has now grown into a celebration of diversity, taste, and togetherness.

Our Mission

To bring people together through bold, aunthentic, and affordable global cuisine. We are committed to celebrating cultural diversity, sopporting local suppliers, and creating a fun, welcoming space where everyone feels at home.
