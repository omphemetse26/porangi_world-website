Our Story

Porangi World was founded in 2021 with a clear vision: to use food as a bridge between cultures. We noticed that people were craving authentic, global flavours but did not always have the means to travel. So we brought the world to Alexandra.

Our goal was to create a welcoming space where guests can explore international tastes, enjoy vibrant dishes, and feel part of a community built on connection and celebration.

From humble beginnings, the brand grew into a foodie destination known for bold flavours, creativity, and a warm, friendly atmosphere.

Our Mission

To bring people together through bold, authentic, and affordable global cuisine. We are committed to celebrating cultural diversity, supporting local suppliers, and creating a fun and welcoming environment where everyone feels at home.

  • Authentic menu inspiration from around the world
  • Fresh ingredients and quality preparation
  • Friendly customer experience in every visit
  • Community-driven hospitality and local pride

Our Values

We believe good food should be memorable, welcoming, and accessible. Every meal reflects our passion for flavour, creativity, and togetherness.

Our Promise

To deliver an experience that is exciting, comforting, and full of personality — one plate at a time.

Our Community

We celebrate Alexandra and the people who make our brand feel alive, vibrant, and truly local.
